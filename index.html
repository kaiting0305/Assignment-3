Manufacture Process

Every piece at MAKES begins with raw sterling silver and a pair of hands. We cut, shape, solder, and polish each item individually in our small studio — no mass production, no shortcuts. The process is slow by design, because we believe the time spent making something is part of what makes it worth wearing. Each piece carries the small marks of that process, and we think that's exactly as it should be.

About Us

MAKES handcrafted jewellery is designed and created on one bench by one woman, Maya Kessler. As the daughter and great-granddaughter of Swiss watchmakers she values the process of creating beautiful unique pieces using jewellers' traditional hand skills.
